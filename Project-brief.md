Which wards in (Ezinihitte Mbaise LGA) are more than 5 km from heath facility?


1. Wards more than 5 km from a health facility may have limited access to healthcare services.
2. Identifying these wards helps locate underserved communities.
3. This supports better planning and equitable healthcare delivery.

The datasets needed
1. Ward boundary shapefile – boundaries of all wards in Ezinihitte Mbaise LGA.
https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria?utm_source

2. Health facility locations – point locations of hospitals, health centres, clinics.
https://healthsites.io/map?country=Nigeria&utm_source

3.LGA boundary – boundary of Ezinihitte Mbaise for defining the study area.
https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-settlement-extents-v4-1/about

4.Road network – roads connecting communities to health facilities.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/?utm_source#map=6/9.12/8.67

5.Population data – population of each ward, if you want to assess how many people are underserved.
https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=74733&utm_source

6. Settlement/community locations – useful for identifying communities far from health facilities.
https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-settlement-extents-v4-1/about


I will build a GIS map showing the wards in Ezinihitte Mbaise LGA and the locations of health facilities.

I will use a 5-km buffer around health facilities to identify wards that are beyond the recommended distance.

The final map will highlight underserved wards to support better healthcare planning and accessibility.
